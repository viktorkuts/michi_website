# Michi — Child Safety Standards

**Effective date:** 25/07/2026

Michi ("Michi", "we", "us") has **zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and
exploitation (CSAE)** and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page sets out
our published standards against CSAE, how we prevent, detect, and act on it, and
how to reach us. It exists to meet the child-safety requirements of the Google
Play and Apple App Store developer policies for apps in the social category.

Michi is an **18+ social app**. Minors are not permitted to create an account or use the Service.

## 1. Our commitment

We prohibit CSAE and CSAM on Michi without exception. This applies to every part
of the Service — profiles, photos, video, audio, usernames, bios, events, group
and private chats, and any other content or conduct. We build, operate, and
moderate Michi to keep this material and the people who would spread it off the
platform, and we act quickly and decisively when we find it.

## 2. What is prohibited

The following are strictly prohibited and will result in immediate removal of the
content, termination of the account, and reporting to the relevant authorities:

- **Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)** — any image, video, or other depiction of
  the sexual abuse or sexual exploitation of a minor, real or computer-generated.
- **Grooming** — contacting, befriending, or building a relationship with a minor
  to enable sexual abuse or exploitation, on or off the platform.
- **Sextortion** — threatening to share intimate imagery to coerce a minor.
- **Sexualization of minors** — any content that sexualizes a person under 18,
  including text, requests, or solicitation.
- **Trafficking and solicitation** — offering, requesting, or facilitating sexual
  contact with, or the sale of, a minor.
- **Off-platform links** — sharing links, handles, or contact details intended to
  distribute CSAM or facilitate any of the above.

## 3. Age requirement

Michi is for adults. **You must be 18 or older to use Michi.** We state this
requirement at sign-up and confirm date of birth on account creation. We do not
knowingly allow anyone under 18 to use the Service. If we learn that an account
belongs to a minor, we remove it and delete the associated personal information.

## 4. How we prevent and detect CSAE

- **Age gating** at sign-up, with a date-of-birth check to confirm users are 18+.
- **Content moderation** — we screen event content before it goes live and review
  reported profiles, media, and messages. Confirmed violations are removed.
- **Reporting and blocking tools** built into the app so users can flag accounts,
  content, and conversations, and block anyone (see Section 5).
- **Account enforcement** — accounts that violate these standards are terminated,
  and we take steps to prevent banned users from returning.
- **Cooperation with authorities** — we preserve relevant evidence and report
  confirmed CSAM and CSAE to the appropriate agencies and law enforcement.

## 5. How to report

If you encounter CSAE or CSAM on Michi, or have any child-safety concern, report
it right away:

- **In the app:** open the profile, content, or chat, tap the menu, and choose
  **Report**. Reports reach a real person, and we prioritize child-safety reports.
- **By email:** write to **[child-safety@synqtogether.com](mailto:child-safety@synqtogether.com)**
  with as much detail as you can (usernames, links, screenshots, and what you saw).

You can also **block** any account from its profile menu — a blocked user can't see
your profile, message you, or join an event you're in.

**If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services
first.**

## 6. What happens after a report

When we receive a report of CSAE or CSAM, we:

1. **Review it promptly** and prioritize it above other reports.
2. **Remove** the offending content and **terminate** the account involved.
3. **Preserve** relevant records to support investigations.
4. **Report** confirmed CSAM and CSAE to the appropriate authorities, including the
   **National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC)** and, in Canada, the
   **Canadian Centre for Child Protection (Cybertip.ca)**, and cooperate with law
   enforcement as required by law.

## 7. Compliance with laws and standards

We handle CSAE in line with applicable Canadian law, including the *Criminal Code*
and the *Act respecting the mandatory reporting of Internet child pornography by
persons who provide an Internet service*, and we cooperate with Canadian and
international authorities. Our standards are designed to comply with the child
safety policies of Google Play and the Apple App Store.

## 8. Child safety point of contact

For questions, reports, or requests from law enforcement and child-safety
organizations relating to CSAE on Michi:

- **E-Mail:** [contact@synqtogether.com](mailto:contact@synqtogether.com)

We aim to acknowledge child-safety reports within **24 hours** and to act on
confirmed violations without delay.
